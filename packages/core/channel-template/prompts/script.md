# Script instructions — __NAME__

The script writer follows this file for every video on this channel. Edit it freely.

## Voice

- Speak to one viewer ("you").
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- No hype words. A number beats an adjective.

## Structure (long video, 8–12 minutes)

1. Hook, first 15 seconds: the surprising fact or the question. No intro, no "welcome back".
2. Why it matters to the viewer.
3. Three to five sections. Each ends with a reason to keep watching.
4. Payoff: answer the hook.
5. One closing line. No begging for likes.

## Facts

- Every number must be real and checkable. Mark anything uncertain with [CHECK].
- No personal financial, legal or medical advice.
